CURATED TEXT RECORD

In December 2025, the FDA approved fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki (T-DXd/Enhertu) with pertuzumab as first-line treatment for adults with unresectable or metastatic HER2-positive breast cancer confirmed by an approved test.

The decision was based on DESTINY-Breast09, a randomized global trial involving adults with HER2-positive advanced or metastatic breast cancer who had not received prior chemotherapy or HER2-targeted therapy for advanced disease, subject to the trial's stated eligibility conditions.
