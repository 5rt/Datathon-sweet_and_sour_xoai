CURATED TEXT RECORD

Breast cancer stage describes how far the cancer has developed and whether it has spread. The stage is determined using the tumour, lymph-node and metastasis (TNM) findings together with tumour grade and biomarker information, including HER2 and hormone-receptor status.

Clinical prognostic staging is assigned before surgery. Pathological prognostic staging is used when surgery is the first treatment and incorporates findings from the removed breast and lymph-node tissue.

PROJECT RELEVANCE

Drug evidence should not be grouped by stage number alone. The extraction schema should retain disease setting, HER2 status, hormone-receptor status, prior treatment and whether treatment is neoadjuvant, adjuvant or metastatic.
