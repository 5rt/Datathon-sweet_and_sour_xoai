CURATED TEXT RECORD

For HER2-positive invasive breast cancer, NICE recommends neoadjuvant chemotherapy where indicated. Pertuzumab with trastuzumab is an option for locally advanced, inflammatory or high-risk early-stage HER2-positive disease.

After surgery, trastuzumab emtansine (T-DM1/Kadcyla) is an option when residual invasive disease remains after neoadjuvant taxane-based and HER2-targeted therapy. Pertuzumab with trastuzumab and chemotherapy is an adjuvant option for node-positive disease. Adjuvant trastuzumab is offered for T1c and larger HER2-positive invasive cancer, and may be considered for T1a/T1b disease after considering comorbidities, prognostic features and toxicity.

The recommendations also call for caution with trastuzumab when there is reduced left-ventricular ejection fraction or clinically important cardiac disease.
